# E2.5 design — bond constraints (RATTLE) in the reference backend

- **State (2026-11-20):**
  - **First step implemented** on engine branch `e25-constraints` (33414cb): RATTLE in velocity
    Verlet with or without NHC/CSVR, the constraint virial, the start projection, bitwise
    restarts.
  - **Evidence:**
    - ideal gas of rigid dimers: molecular pressure at every step to 5.5·10⁻⁵;
    - rigid triangles: constraints to 10⁻¹⁰, energy to 10⁻⁴ over 20 000 steps at 2 fs;
    - two rigid SPC/Fw molecules through the CLI: total energy to 0.4 % of the 19 kJ/mol
      exchanged;
    - 111 workspace tests.
  - **Step 2 (882d1b3): C-rescale with constraints by molecular scaling.**
    - Atomic scaling followed by SHAKE broke the barostat's energy bookkeeping: H varied by
      17.5 kJ/mol against 0.16 kJ/mol for a stiff bond.
    - Molecules with constraints are now shifted with their centre of mass. Pressure and
      bookkeeping use the virial conjugate to that scaling.
    - Result: H varies by 0.012 kJ/mol.
    - Unconstrained runs stay bit for bit unchanged.
  - **Still refused with a clear error:** Langevin BAOAB, minimization, and the OpenMM bridge
    with constraints.
- **Origin:** draft of 2026-11-20 (design E2.9, P7). Written at the author's request to continue
  the engine in the approved order. As with E2.7, the work may start alongside this draft.
  **Every decision below is open for review.**
- **Why:**
  - Model constraints are part of spec 0.1: `constraints` in a molecule type; rigid water from
    a GROMACS `[ settles ]`.
  - Published force-field results are often computed with constrained bonds. Caleman 2012
    constrained every bond (rung EC). Rigid water needs constraints.
  - Until 2026-11-20 the engine counted constraints in N_dof but did not enforce them. They are
    refused since then (engine d9a6b19).

## 1. Algorithm

**RATTLE** (Andersen, J. Comput. Phys. 52, 24 (1983)) inside the existing velocity-Verlet
splitting. Each constraint is |r_i − r_j| = d_ij, in the minimum image.

1. **Positions (SHAKE step), after every drift A.**
   - Iterate over the constraints in package order.
   - Each correction moves i and j along the old bond vector, weighted by inverse mass, until
     every |r_ij² − d²|/d² < tol.
   - The same displacement divided by the drift time is added to the velocities.
2. **Velocities, after the last operator that changes them.** Those are the final kick, the
   thermostat and the COM removal. Iterate until every |v_ij · r_ij|/(d·v_scale) < tol.
3. **Tolerance:** `tol = 1e-10` relative (PLAN.md E2.5).
   - No convergence within 1000 iterations is an error, not a warning (invariant 7). The error
     names the constraint.
4. **Order:** fixed, single-threaded. The cost is small against the forces, and the result stays
   bitwise reproducible (ADR 0010).

## 2. Place in the step (invariant 10)

The constraints are an operator, `ucmd-md::constraints`, applied after A and at the end of the
step. They are never code inside `kick` or `drift`.
- **Velocity Verlet + NHC / CSVR:**
  - O(½) B(½) A(1) **S** F B(½) O(½) **R**.
  - Thermostat scaling preserves v_ij·r_ij = 0. The final R handles the COM removal and
    rounding.
- **C-rescale (as implemented, step 2):**
  - molecules with constraints are scaled by their centre of mass (rigid shift, only the
    centre-of-mass velocity scaled), every other atom atomically (E2.7);
  - pressure and energy bookkeeping use the conjugate virial: W_provider − Σ (r_i − R_I) ⊗ F_i
    for rigid molecules, with their centre-of-mass kinetic tensor;
  - the first idea, atomic scaling followed by S, failed the conservation test (17.5 against
    0.16 kJ/mol).
- **Langevin BAOAB:** S after each A(½), R after O. That is the constrained BAOAB of Leimkuhler
  & Matthews, Proc. R. Soc. A 472, 20160138 (2016). It is refused in the first version
  (`E_UNSUPPORTED`) and comes second.
- **Start:**
  - the package positions are projected (S) and the generated velocities projected (R), with
    the largest correction logged;
  - a correction above 1 % of a constraint length is an error: the package geometry does not
    fit its constraints.
- **Minimizer (FIRE):** S after each move, R on the FIRE velocity. Second step; refused until
  then.

## 3. Virial and pressure

- The constraint forces contribute W_c = Σ_c r_ij ⊗ g_ij (the same sign convention as the
  provider virial, units.md §4).
  - From the position step: g = 2 m_i Δx_i / dt² for a full-step drift.
  - From the velocity step: g = m_i Δv_i / (dt/2).
- W_c is added to the provider virial before the pressure, the barostat and the pressure-tensor
  columns use it.
- **Check:** an ideal gas of rigid dimers (no forces between them) has ⟨P⟩ = N_mol k_B T / V
  exactly. The atomic kinetic pressure alone gives 2 N_mol k_B T / V: the constraint virial
  must remove the difference.

## 4. Tests (test-first)

| Test | Pass |
|---|---|
| Rigid dimers, triangles (rigid water) in NVE | every constraint to 1e-10 after every step; v_ij·r_ij = 0 |
| NVE energy conservation, rigid water box, 2 fs | drift no worse than OpenMM with identical settings (PLAN.md E2.5) |
| Ideal gas of rigid dimers, NVT | ⟨P⟩ = N_mol k_B T/V and ⟨K⟩ = N_dof k_B T/2 within errors |
| Restart | bitwise identical (as every integrator) |
| NPT with constraints | conserved quantity holds; density vs OpenMM (constraints, MC barostat) within the combined error |
| Non-convergence | an impossible constraint set gives an error naming the constraint |
| Bridge | OpenMM gets the same constraints (`addConstraint`, `addConstrainPositions/Velocities` in the CustomIntegrators); tolerance 1e-10 in double, 1e-6 in mixed precision (stated in the provenance) |

## 5. Not in this task

- SETTLE (analytic rigid water). RATTLE converges for water in a few iterations; SETTLE is a
  later optimisation.
- Angle constraints. Spec 0.1 constrains distances only; rigid water uses three distances.
