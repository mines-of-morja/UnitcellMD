# E2.9 design — the validation ladder: reproduce published behaviour, one problem at a time

- **Status:** draft, 2026-11-20. Written at the author's request: "resolve the problems one by
  one, run short dynamics, validations … continue with the build of the engine … reproduce
  certain behaviour". As with E2.7, the work may start alongside this draft. **Every decision
  below is open for the author's review**, and the items marked ❓ wait for the author.
- **Why:** the pilot (report §3.5) showed that the stack works end to end, but its numbers are
  not yet tested against independent results. The force field fails in a specific way (EC/EMC
  densities, EC kept out of the Li⁺ shell), and several observables have no tool or no
  reference yet. Each rung below fixes one problem and ends with a check that can fail.

## 2. The rungs

**Rung W — SPC/Fw water** (pre-approved for fixtures; builder library `water-spcfw`).
- **System:** 500 molecules (2.47 nm box, cutoff 0.9 nm, PME 10⁻⁵, LJ tail correction), 298.15 K,
  0.5 fs. A 1000-molecule box checks finite size (Yeh–Hummer needs two box sizes or η).
- **Engine check (hard):** CPU engine vs OpenMM backend, same package.
  - NPT 0.25 ns: densities within the combined block uncertainty;
  - NVT 20 ps NVE drift of the CPU engine reported.
- **Behaviour check (against Wu et al. 2006):** ρ, D (corrected to infinite size), ε, ΔH_vap,
  and η once P5 is resolved. Targets and tolerances are taken from the paper's tables when it is
  in `../Lit`; until then they are blank, not guessed.
- **Before production:** the transcribed parameters ("verify before production use") are
  checked against Table I of the paper.

**Rung EC — pure EC at 39 °C** (PLAN.md §8 rung 5): LigParGen (current) and, if the author
allows the test, the OPLS-AA atom types (Caleman 2012). The latter reproduces a published
GROMACS result and so checks the engine and the set together. It is evidence for D1, not the
decision.

**Rung SPC/E — rigid water** after E2.5: published SPC/E properties as a second target, and the
first test of constraints.

**Later rungs** (LiPF₆ in EC, the electrolyte) are A4 and stage C of the calculation plan.

## 3. New analysis (ucmd-analysis)

- **Dielectric constant** (A7), static, from the fluctuation of the total dipole M = Σ qᵢrᵢ.
  - Molecules must be whole; neutral molecules make M independent of the origin.
  - With Ewald/PME (conducting boundary): ε = 1 + (⟨M²⟩ − ⟨M⟩²)/(3ε₀Vk_BT), block errors.
  - Validated on synthetic data with a known answer first.
  - Slow convergence is expected: water needs ≥ 5 ns.
- **ΔH_vap** (A5):
  - ΔH_vap = ⟨U_gas⟩ − ⟨U_liq⟩/N + RT (ideal gas; no polarization correction unless the
    reference applies one).
  - ⟨U_gas⟩ comes from single-molecule runs (non-periodic, Langevin). For a purely harmonic
    molecule like SPC/Fw it equals the equipartition value, which tests the workflow.
- Both report the inputs with the result (window, blocks, N, V).

## 4. Pressure tensor output ❓ (spec change after `spec-v0.1.0`)

- Green–Kubo η needs the off-diagonal pressure P_xy, P_xz, P_yz every few steps.
- **Proposal for spec v0.1.1:** optional thermo columns `pressure_xy_bar` … `pressure_yz_bar`
  (molecular or atomic virial stated in the header); the engine and the bridge write them on
  request (`[output.thermo] pressure_tensor = true`).
- Backwards compatible: old files simply lack the columns.

## 5. Study driver (tools/study)

- **Stall timeout:** 30 → 5 min. Thermo is written about every second of wall time on the GPU.
- **Checkpoints:** every 0.1 ns in GPU studies (2 MB each).
- **Hang snapshot** (as the telemetry logger) written by the driver before it kills the engine.
- **Acceptance:** driver tests with a fake engine that stops writing.

## 6. E2.5 constraints (sketch; its own design before code)

- RATTLE in the operator scheme (invariant 10).
- Bonds to H plus rigid water, DOF reduction, tolerance 10⁻¹⁰ relative.
- **Acceptance (PLAN.md):** SPC/E at 2 fs drifts no worse than OpenMM.

## 7. Order of work

1. P1 when the GPU is free; P2 meanwhile.
2. P4: the tools, test-first on synthetic data.
3. Rung W on the CPU (density) while A4 runs; then on the GPU (ε, D).
4. P5 and P6 when the author has answered.
5. P7 (E2.5), then P8 with an agreed target.
