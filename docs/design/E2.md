## 2. Topology: exclusions and 1-4 pairs (E2.2)

- **Per molecule type, at `initialize`:** shortest bond-path distances d by BFS (the spec's
  rule, system-package.md §5.2).
  - d ≤ 2: excluded.
  - d = 3: a 1-4 pair.
  - Rings count by their shortest path.
- **Expansion to global indices per instance, stored two ways:**
  - per atom, a sorted list of partners with d ≤ 3, in CSR layout. The pair loop skips these
    partners;
  - explicit lists of excluded pairs (for the Ewald/PME corrections) and of 1-4 pairs, with
    precomputed σᵢⱼ, s_LJ·εᵢⱼ and s_C·qᵢqⱼ.
- **Check:** at `initialize`, bonded atoms must be closer than half the smallest cell height,
  the spec's E_MOLECULE_SPLIT rule.
  - Positions stay unwrapped, so molecules stay whole, and bonded terms need no minimum
    image (PLAN.md D11).

## 3. Bonded terms (E2.1)

| Term | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| bond | ½k(r − r₀)² | |
| angle | ½k(θ − θ₀)² | θ = atan2(\|u×v\|, u·v), accurate near 0 and π; a 180° angle with θ₀ = 180° gives zero force |
| dihedral | periodic k[1 + cos(nφ − φ₀)]; OPLS Fourier; Ryckaert–Bellemans with ψ = φ − π | φ by atan2 with the IUPAC sign convention (as GROMACS and OpenMM); forces by the Blondel–Karplus formulas |
| improper | periodic; harmonic ½k(ξ − ξ₀)² with ξ − ξ₀ wrapped into (−π, π] | the atom order as given; the central atom is wherever the source put it |

- **Energy terms:** `bond`, `angle`, `dihedral`, `improper`.
- **Virial:** W = Σ rᵢ ⊗ Fᵢ per term. Each term's forces sum to zero, so the origin does not
  matter, and whole molecules make it exact in periodic boxes.
- **Tests:**
  - the test kit per term on random geometries (finite-difference forces and strain virial,
    momentum, determinism);
  - single EC, EMC and PF₆⁻ molecules against oracle v1, per term, to 10⁻⁸ relative (PLAN
    E2.1).

## 4. Nonbonded: LJ, 1-4 pairs, Coulomb (E2.2, E2.3)

- **One neighbour list** (E1's, now filtering excluded partners) serves LJ and real-space
  Coulomb, with one cutoff, as in Lehnert's 1.4 nm.
- **LJ:**
  - both combination rules, through the per-type-pair table of E1;
  - `truncate` or `potential-shift`;
  - the optional mixture tail correction of E1 (homogeneous approximation, all pairs, as in
    GROMACS and OpenMM).
- **1-4 pairs:** computed without cutoff and without shift (GROMACS `[ pairs ]`, OpenMM
  exceptions):
  - LJ: 4·s_LJ·ε[(σ/r)¹² − (σ/r)⁶];
  - Coulomb: s_C·k_e·qq/r.
- **Coulomb**, provider key `coulomb`:
  - `direct`: all pairs, no cutoff; non-periodic packages only. Exact; for molecules and
    clusters.
  - `ewald`: classic Ewald (real + reciprocal + self + corrections). The reference for PME
    validation.
  - `pme`: smooth PME (§5), for production.
- **Ewald and PME corrections:**
  - The reciprocal part covers all pairs, so every excluded pair gets −k_e·qq·erf(αr)/r.
  - A 1-4 pair gets the same correction, plus its scaled plain Coulomb.
  - The self energy is −k_e·α/√π·Σq².
  - Periodic packages are neutral by spec (E_NET_CHARGE); the provider checks this to 10⁻⁶ e
    and never adds a neutralizing background.
- **Ewald splitting α:**
  - from `ewald_tolerance` with OpenMM's rule, α = √(−ln(2·tol)) / r_c, so that matched
    comparisons are exact;
  - or set directly with `ewald_alpha_per_nm`.
- **Energy terms:** `lj`, `lj14`, `coulomb` (real + reciprocal + self + corrections, including
  the scaled 1-4 part). This is exactly the split of oracle v1. The Coulomb parts appear as
  observables for diagnosis.
- **Tests:**
  - test kit on small periodic and non-periodic systems;
  - water-2 against the NumPy reference of oracle v1;
  - NaCl Madelung constant 1.747565 to 10⁻⁸ with Ewald (PLAN E2.3);
  - EC/EMC term decomposition against oracle v1 (PLAN E2.2).

## 5. Smooth PME (E2.4)

- **Method:** Essmann et al., J. Chem. Phys. 103, 8577 (1995), with cardinal B-splines of order
  p ∈ {4, 5, 6}. The default is p = 5, the order OpenMM uses, so that matched comparisons are
  exact.
- **Grid:** `pme_grid = [nx, ny, nz]`, or from the tolerance with OpenMM's rule
  n = ⌈2αL / (3·tol^(1/5))⌉, rounded up to 2ᵃ3ᵇ5ᶜ7ᵈ.
- **Cell:** any reduced triclinic cell (spec form), using fractional coordinates.
- **Reciprocal virial:** the analytic expression of Essmann et al. in the spec's convention
  W = −∂E/∂ε (units.md §4).
- **FFT:** 3D, with `rustfft` 1D plans along each axis on a complex grid. Real-to-complex is an
  E2.8 optimization.
- **Tests (PLAN E2.4):**
  - against converged classic Ewald, on NaCl and on the LP57 box: rms relative force error
    ≤ 10⁻⁵ at default settings, converging with grid and order;
  - against OpenMM PME with matched α, grid and order (oracle v1 `pme_parameters`) on the
    builder's LP57 package: energy to 10⁻⁶, forces to 10⁻⁵;
  - test kit on a small charged periodic system: finite-difference forces and strain virial.

## 6. Provider kinds and keys

`[[providers]] kind = "force-field"` uses the package's parameters for every term. Internally it
combines the bonded and nonbonded parts with one shared neighbour list.

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `cutoff_nm` | required for periodic systems | LJ and real-space Coulomb |
| `lj_cutoff_mode` | `"truncate"` | or `"potential-shift"` |
| `dispersion_correction` | `false` | LJ tail correction (energy and pressure) |
| `coulomb` | `"pme"` (periodic), `"direct"` (non-periodic) | or `"ewald"` |
| `ewald_tolerance` | `1e-5` | sets α (and the PME grid if none is given) |
| `ewald_alpha_per_nm`, `pme_grid`, `pme_order` | from the tolerance; order 5 | explicit PME parameters |
| `neighbor_skin_nm` | `0.2` | Verlet skin, as in E1 |

- The defaults follow the benchmark: a plain LJ cutoff and no tail correction, as in Lehnert's
  LAMMPS set-up.
- Lehnert's PPPM accuracy of 10⁻⁵ is a relative force accuracy. Our tolerance has the
  OpenMM/GROMACS meaning, so equal numbers are comparable, not identical.
- `bonded` and `nonbonded` are the same parts as separate kinds, for modular mixing (ADR 0008).
  Example: `bonded` plus an ML or QM provider for everything else.

## 7. FIRE minimizer and `ucmd minimize` (E2.0)

- **Algorithm:** FIRE 2.0 (Guénolé et al., Comput. Mater. Sci. 175, 109584 (2020)), with the
  semi-implicit Euler integrator and the parameters of that paper (checked against it during
  implementation).
- **Scope:** provider-agnostic; it works with any provider, ML and QM included. Physical
  masses; the cell is fixed.
- **Command:** `ucmd minimize run.toml --out minimized.ucsys [--max-force F] [--max-steps N]`.
  - It uses the package and providers of `run.toml`, ignoring the integrator.
  - It writes a new package with provenance: start and end energy, max |F|, steps.
  - If it has not converged, it exits non-zero and writes nothing (errors propagate).
  - Proposed defaults: F = 100 kJ mol⁻¹ nm⁻¹, N = 10000.
  - A command rather than a `[minimize]` section avoids a spec change. It fits the chain
    package → minimize → package → run.
- **Tests:**
  - LJ₇ and LJ₁₃ clusters reach their known minima, −16.505384 ε and −44.326801 ε (Wales &
    Doye, J. Phys. Chem. A 101, 5111 (1997));
  - the packed LP57 box goes from max |F| ≈ 1.7×10⁴ to below 100 kJ mol⁻¹ nm⁻¹.

## 8. Thermostats (E2.6)

- **Nosé–Hoover chains:**
  - Martyna, Tuckerman, Tobias, Klein, Mol. Phys. 87, 1117 (1996); the chain is propagated as the O
    operator of PLAN.md §2.5, with Suzuki–Yoshida weights.
  - Masses Q₁ = N_f·k_B·T·τ² and Qⱼ = k_B·T·τ². This is LAMMPS's `fix nvt` with Tdamp = τ, so
    Lehnert's 100 fs is `tau_ps = 0.1`. Chain length 3 (spec default).
  - The conserved quantity is reported: H = K + U + Σ p²/2Q + N_f·kT·ξ₁ + kT·Σⱼ≥₂ ξⱼ.
  - The chain state goes into the checkpoint (v2), and restarts stay bitwise.
- **Langevin BAOAB:**
  - Leimkuhler & Matthews (2013); `integrator = "langevin-baoab"` with `kind = "langevin"`.
  - The O step is the exact Ornstein–Uhlenbeck update with c = exp(−γΔt), driven by a seeded
    stream. The heat exchanged is accumulated for the conserved quantity.
- **Tests:**
  - canonical ⟨K⟩ and Var(K) for argon, for both thermostats (as E1.6);
  - NHC conserved-quantity drift;
  - exact configurational sampling of a harmonic oscillator with BAOAB;
  - bitwise restart with each thermostat.

## 9. Proposed: deterministic multithreading now (from E2.8)

- **Why now:** single-threaded, the 7036-atom box with a 1.4 nm cutoff has about 5×10⁶ pairs
  per step. That is a rough estimate of 0.2–0.4 s per step. The GUI preview's short runs would
  then take hours instead of minutes.
- **How:**
  - `rayon` over fixed-size chunks of the pair list and of the PME charge spreading;
  - per-chunk force buffers, summed in a fixed order;
  - the thread count from the machine profile (`threads`).
- **Criterion:** results are bitwise identical for 1, 4 and 16 threads. This is stronger than
  E2.8's 10⁻¹².
- The remaining optimizations stay in E2.8 (real-to-complex FFT, tabulated kernels, SIMD,
  benchmark), after agreeing a target with the author.

## 9a. The same results on any hardware (ADR 0010)

- **Task E2.R, first:** make the reference backend bitwise reproducible across machines.
  - `libm` replaces the standard library's `exp` in CSVR.
  - Own normal and gamma samplers built on `libm` replace `rand_distr`.
  - PME uses rustfft's `FftPlannerScalar`.
  - The other rules (no `target-cpu=native`, fixed-order reductions) are kept.
- **Golden-hash test:**
  - a fixed argon NVT run with CSVR and velocity generation; the SHA-256 of the final
    positions and velocities is committed;
  - CI on GitHub's machines must reproduce the hash computed on the author's workstation.
  - The test is extended to the molecular force field once PME exists.
- **Provenance:** every output records the backend (`cpu-reference`), the thread count and the
  CPU model.

## 10. Milestone M2a

**Chain:**
1. `ucmd-build build examples/lp57-1M.toml`;
2. `ucmd minimize`;
3. `ucmd run`: NVT with NHC τ = 0.1 ps, 1 fs steps, PME at 1.4 nm, 10 ps;
4. single points against OpenMM.

**Criteria:**
- **Single points** of the LP57 package before and after minimization against oracle v1 with
  matched PME: total and per-term energies to 10⁻⁶ relative, forces to 10⁻⁵ relative (rms).
- **The 10 ps run** completes:
  - the temperature is held, and the conserved-quantity drift is reported;
  - a restart in the middle is bitwise identical.
- **Threads:** results do not depend on the thread count.

The report goes to `validation/m2a/REPORT.md`.
