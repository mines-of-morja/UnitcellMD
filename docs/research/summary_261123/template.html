Summary

  • Aim, unchanged. Predict κ, t₊ and D of LiPF₆ in carbonate solvents from molecular dynamics and test them against the measurements of Landesfeind & Gasteiger [1]. No model parameter is fitted to transport data.
  • Done since 21 November. The software reproduces a published water model [8] in ${q_wu_agree} properties compared. A better EC model (M1) was chosen at 1 M by a test fixed in advance. The reference frames of t₊ are settled. The functional for the quantum-chemistry clusters is chosen, and the cluster calculations are running.
  • The Li⁺ shell has improved and is not right yet. In its first shell, Li⁺ now has ${t2_M1_f_ec} EC instead of ${t2_M0_f_ec}. Dielectric spectroscopy [3] gives at most about ${bound}. Up to ${t2_deficit} EC are still missing.
  • Transport is where it was. κ has been measured only for the baseline (M0): ${kappa} S/m against ${kappa_exp} S/m. In short runs, M1 leaves the Nernst–Einstein κ unchanged within its error. No predictive model exists yet, so the κ pass criteria have not been applied.
  • Next: a first-shell correction fitted to quantum-chemistry clusters (running), then viscosity and charge scaling, and only then the transport campaign.

4. Why EC is still missing, and the correction under way (trial T6)

The force fields do not prefer EC enough. MP2 gives a difference of ${d_mp2} kcal/mol, M0 ${d_m0} and M1 ${d_m1}. M1 has the right sign but only ${d_m1_pct} % of the size. The absolute energies at MP2 are ${qm_ec} (EC) and ${qm_emc} (EMC) kcal/mol. A single molecule in vacuum overstates the preference in the liquid [5], so the reference for the correction is a full shell: Li⁺(EC)n(EMC)4−n, n = 0…4. Two corrections compete: (A) one new Li⁺ size, and (B) separate Li⁺–O terms for EC and EMC. Both will be fitted to the clusters and then tested in the liquid against the dielectric bound.

  • A limit to know about. All three functionals bind Li⁺ more strongly than MP2. The energy uncertainty used in the fit is therefore ${t6_s_E} kcal/mol, against ${t6_s_d} Å for distances, so the energies will constrain option B only weakly.
  • State. The five cluster optimizations were restarted on 23 November with a more robust optimizer setting. No cluster has finished, and no fit or liquid run exists yet. The energies follow, then about ${q_t6_gpu} of GPU time per option.

5. Transport: where it stands

The only transport run is still the baseline M0 (20 ns NVT, 1 M, 20 °C, ${pilot_frames} frames, five blocks; Onsager analysis [7]). Its κ has not changed; what is new is t₊ in the frame of each reference.

  • κ. ${kappa} S/m, a factor ${kappa_ratio} below experiment; the Nernst–Einstein value ${kappa_ne} S/m is a factor ${kne_ratio} low. So the ions are too slow; association does not explain the deficit. Whether the solvent is too viscous or Li⁺ binds too strongly is trial T4.
  • t₊ frames (trial T1). SOL, with EC and EMC as one solvent weighted by mass, is compared with Landesfeind; VOL with eNMR; COM with other MD. The same run gives ${tp_sol} (SOL), ${tp_vol} (VOL) and ${tp_com} (COM). The choice of ion volumes [9] moves the VOL value by ${t1_dab}, far below the error. The conversion between frames holds to ${t1_eq2}. The VOL value uses the salt volume from the density fit of [1], which has been read once.
  • What cannot be said. With errors of ${tp_sem_lo}–${tp_sem_hi}, t₊ lies within ${tp_max_pull} standard errors of every published value and separates none. Reaching 10 % needs about ${q_noise_tplus} per state point for t₊ and ${q_noise_kappa} for κ.

7. Next steps

  • T6: cluster energies, the two fits, then three 3 ns liquid runs per option. If a candidate overshoots the dielectric bound, that is reported and nothing is refitted to the liquid. If neither passes, explicit polarization is the next level.
  • T3–T5: density protocol, viscosity against Li⁺ binding, charge scaling from the refractive index. EMC, whose density is off by +${dens_emc} %, is the other solvent to improve.
  • Then transport for the model that passes: κ(c, T), t₊, D and the thermodynamic factor. The criteria stay as fixed on 21 November: κ(1 M, 20 °C) within ±15 % and the κ maximum within ±0.2 M, without refit. A failure is reported as a result.
  • Publication: a software paper for J. Chem. Inf. Model., not before May 2027. Before that, a production set: tagged releases, at least four independent runs per model.
  • What would help most: the measured points behind [1] with their uncertainties and the density data, sourced refractive indices of EC and EMC, and Raman or NMR data on the Li⁺ shell in EC:EMC.
